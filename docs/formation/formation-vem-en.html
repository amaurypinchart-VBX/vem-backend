The 6 golden rules

1
One project = one internal number
In Viewbox format (e.g. 9999DEMOVIE). Without an internal number, the project cannot receive e-mails.
2
Everything goes through VEM
Files, drawings, bookings: in the project, through the form or by e-mail.
3
Check what an e-mail creates

The checklist day by day
It replaces tasks and is required before the handover can be signed.

One daily per site day
With photos: it is the memory of the project and the basis of the report.

Common mistakes

  • Internal number empty or in the wrong format → e-mails do not find the project.
