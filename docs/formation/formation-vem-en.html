✏️ Saisie entrée par entrée📋 Import texte libre (IA)🎙️ Dictée vocale
ModeHowBest for
✏️ Saisie entrée par entrée (entry by entry)Time + « Décrivez ce qui a été fait... » (describe what was done) + « + Ajouter ». The time moves on by 30 min.Writing as the day goes
📋 Import texte libre (IA) (free text)Paste your notes, 🤖 Analyser avec l'IA → Extraire les entrées, check, ✅ Valider et ajouter au journal.Notes taken on the phone or on WhatsApp
🎙️ Dictée vocale (voice)🎙️ Démarrer la dictée, speak, then 🤖 Analyser avec l'IA; or 🎵 Choisir un fichier audio (50 MB max).End of day, hands busy
ℹ️ Good to know
The AI splits the text into time-stamped entries (without inventing times) and returns the text in French. Always read it again before validating.

Chapter 11 · Daily Reports

Notes, photos and attachments

  1. « Notes générales / Résumé » (general notes): incidents, important points.
  2. « 📸 Prendre / Ajouter photos »: select all photos at once.
  3. « 📎 Pièces jointes » (attachments): tick the checklist to attach (current state), add PDF / photos / Word / Excel (10 MB max per file).
⚠️ Watch out
On a phone, a new photo selection replaces the previous one. Pick them all together, or add more later when editing.
ℹ️ Good to know
Attached PDFs and photos are added, readable, at the end of the report PDF. Word / Excel stay as links.
VEM · Training5
Do not fill in « ✅ Avancement tâches »: tasks are no longer used.

  • « 💾 Sauvegarder » (Save): the report is saved, nothing is sent.
  • « 📤 Sauv. & Envoyer » (Save & Send): saves, then opens the sending window.
  • The PDF is sent as an attachment, in the chosen language.
⚠️ Watch out
The client is ticked by default. Untick it if the report is internal. After sending, the « 📤 Envoyer » button disappears: it cannot be sent again.

Chapter 11 · Daily Reports

Review, edit, PDF

  1. Project tab 📓 Daily Reports: 👁️ read, ✏️ edit, 📄 PDF.
  2. When editing: « 🤖 Assistant IA » adds entries from a new text or audio (nothing is replaced).
  3. Photo caption: ✏️ on the photo.
  4. 📄 PDF: choose « 🇫🇷 Français » or « 🇬🇧 English » (texts translated automatically).
⚠️ Watch out
When editing, the weather « 🌩️ Orage » (Storm) is not offered: an edited « Orage » report goes back to Sunny. Deleting all entries is not saved.
⛔ Not allowed
Do not delete a report: correct it.
VEM · Training7
The PDF contains the client contact block: keep it in mind before sending it to a third party.

Exercise — the daily of 15/03

  1. Create the daily of the demo project for 15/03/2027: sunny, 6 workers.
  2. Dictate or paste: « 7:45 arrival, 9:15 crane, assembly of the first 3 boxes, 12:00 break ».
  3. Add 2 photos (in a single selection) and attach the assembly checklist.
  4. Save without sending, then download the PDF in English.
Key points
  • One daily per day, with photos.
  • The AI structures, you review.
  • Client ticked by default when sending.
  • No resending after sending.
